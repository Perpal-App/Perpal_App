Decision report · 8 August 2026 · evidence checked against current public documentation

Replace Flash with Pacifica

Do not restore Velocity as the production venue yet. Pacifica is the strongest currently integrable Solana perps candidate for PerPal's Android and private-wallet flow.

Recommendation
Pacifica is now the sole implemented provider. Flash code and its SDK were removed in one vertical migration. Keep T as the user-facing trading identity and sign every order message locally on T after confirmation. Physical Android confirmation remains pending.

Why Pacifica

  • Public REST and WebSocket APIs expose markets, prices, candles, order book, trades, positions, orders, funding, and account state.
  • Orders use deterministic JSON plus a local Ed25519 signature, which fits T without bundling a Node-only protocol SDK.
  • Market, limit, stop, reduce-only, TP/SL, edit, and cancel flows are documented.
  • The venue reports 65+ markets; its market-info endpoint is the source of truth for leverage, tick size, lot size, and order limits.
  • DefiLlama currently reports about $10.8B 30-day perp volume, $28.2M TVL, and $105M open interest.

Architecture impact

  1. Keep Privy M, Umbra funding, T derivation/storage, and the TradingView chart shell.
  2. Use Pacifica market info, prices, mark candles, signed orders, portfolio, deposit, and withdrawal APIs.
  3. Deposit collateral from T to Pacifica, sign confirmed orders locally, stream fills/positions, then request withdrawal back to T before the Umbra private withdrawal.
  4. Flash SDK, ER/data/stats configuration, internal fee signer, settlement provider, screens, hooks, and active copy have been removed.

Conflicts and limits

  • Pacifica uses an off-chain matching engine plus a Solana hot/cold bridge. It is not the same trust model as every trade executing directly in an on-chain program.
  • Pacifica standard deposits are USDC-only. PerPal may hold USDT in T and converts only the required amount to USDC before deposit.
  • Umbra protects the M-to-T funding link only within its assumptions. Pacifica can observe T's account activity, and T's deposit/withdrawal activity remains public on Solana.
  • The old sole-provider decision is superseded and no retired provider integration remains executable.

Required proof before release confirmation

One physical-Android test with a fresh T: fetch public markets, sign an authenticated action, deposit the minimum USDC, open and close the minimum position, cancel an order, withdraw to T, then resume the Umbra private-withdrawal path. Static implementation is complete; this real-money confirmation is not.
